AI Console Hub: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who this applies to

AI Console Hub is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

The app requests these Google permissions, all read-only:

  • gmail.readonly: to search and read the owner's email and its attachments
  • calendar.readonly: to list the owner's calendar events
  • openid and email: to know which Google account was signed in

How it is used

Email and calendar data is read only when the owner asks a question that needs it, and only to answer that question. The app cannot send, modify or delete email or calendar events.

Storage and sharing

  • Only the OAuth refresh tokens are stored, in a permission-restricted file on the owner's private home server.
  • Email and calendar content is not stored, sold, or used for advertising.
  • To answer a question, relevant excerpts may be sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) used by the assistant. They are not shared with anyone else.

AI Console Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the server also removes access.
