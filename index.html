AI Console Hub

AI Console Hub is a private, personal assistant run by a single individual on their own home server. It is not a public service and does not accept outside users.

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events (read-only) so it can answer the owner's questions, for example "what's on my calendar today?" or "did I get the invoice email?".

It never sends, edits or deletes email or calendar events.
